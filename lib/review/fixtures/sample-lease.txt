RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement is entered into between Oakridge Property LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Lee ("Tenant") for the premises at 412 Maple Street, Unit 2B.

ARTICLE 1. Parties

Landlord and Tenant are the only parties to this lease.

ARTICLE IV
QUIET ENJOYMENT

Tenant may quietly enjoy the premises as long as Tenant pays what is owed.

ARTICLE 5
RENT

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,150.00 on the first day of each month.

Section 8. Security Deposit

Tenant shall deposit $3,225.00 as a security deposit before receiving the keys.

12. Assignment

Tenant shall not assign this lease or sublet the premises without Landlord's prior written consent.

Section 14 - Early Termination

If Tenant vacates before the end of the term, Tenant remains liable for the remaining rent.
